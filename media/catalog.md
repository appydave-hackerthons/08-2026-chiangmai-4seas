## 2026-08-01-cm4s-x-whiteboard-funnel.jpg

**Tags:** `whiteboard` `diagram` `establishing` `cm4s-x`

**What it shows:** The discovery-to-selection funnel drawn on the office whiteboard. Three
ownership lanes down the left marked `D E K`. Down the middle: list questions and goals, a boxed
`FACT SHEET`, then `Elaborations` and `OP CARDS`. To the right, two rows of roughly nine empty
boxes — the candidate set. Below, `Judge`, then about five boxes with ticks and one marked `HOT`,
with arrows dropping into a segmented bar at the bottom: the build lane.

**Composition:** Landscape phone photo, whiteboard fills the right two-thirds. Blue marker on
white. Office visible at the left edge — desks, a chair, an air-con unit. Slight glare top-left,
handwriting legible but not crisp. Wide margins around the drawing; a crop to the board alone
reads much stronger.

**Usability:** Strong establishing shot for "here's how we thought about it" — genuine, unstaged,
and it carries the whole method in one frame. Best used early in a cut, cropped to the board and
possibly with the stages highlighted in sequence as a build-on. Not usable as a legible reference
on its own; the transcription in `docs/board-2026-08-01.md` is what people should read.

**Caveats:** Some words are illegible in the photo — do not burn a transcription over it without
checking that doc. Nothing sensitive in frame.
