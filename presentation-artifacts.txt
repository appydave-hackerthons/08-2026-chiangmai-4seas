1 · THE APPLICATION — interview-copilot
================================================================================

What it is
  A local-first research partner that turns a live interview into an evidence
  base while the conversation is still happening. Five specialist lenses —
  clarification, opportunity, bias guard, memory, research — read one shared
  evidence pool and surface the next useful question. The human stays in charge;
  the software never joins the conversation.

Repository (public)
  https://github.com/appydave-hackerthons/interview-copilot

The workspace — what the interviewer sees
  https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f713482e-164b-4e16-a7fa-7771aa2ad4ea
  Conversation path, live transcript, evidence pool, and the five lenses
  surfacing live guidance cards.

The report it produced — what comes out
  https://claude.ai/code/artifact/97b27347-2995-4725-ae67-7fbf7c90f167
  Pains, key facts, verbatim quotes, unanswered questions, a labelled
  hypothesis and one validation test.

Built by
  Ethan Ooi

From one real 20-minute interview
  157 transcript turns
  45 evidence items extracted
  10 pain points · 24 key facts · 10 verbatim quotes · 12 unanswered questions
  Coverage 4 of 6 criteria — the app marked its own interview incomplete
  and said which two criteria were missed.

5 · THE ONE-LINE PITCH
================================================================================

People already produce valuable traces — conversations, notes, coding sessions.
This system listens to them, discovers opportunities nobody formally requested,
lets a human choose which matter, and turns the chosen ones into working
software.

The first thing it built was a better version of its own first step.
