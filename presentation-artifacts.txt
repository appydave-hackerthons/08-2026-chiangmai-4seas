WHAT WE BUILT

A live research partner for interviews. It listens while the conversation is
happening, turns it into a structured evidence base, and quietly suggests the
next question worth asking. The human stays in charge.

One 20-minute interview produced 45 pieces of evidence, 10 pain points, and a
scored opportunity — ProofPass, 18/20.
