What we built

A Dynamic Interview Copilot

A real-time AI guide that helps problem solvers conduct better customer interviews — even if they've never done customer development before.

Nimman Hackathon · August 2026 · Chiang Mai

The problem

Problem solvers don't know how to ask

  • They know the themes — wellness, connection, community
  • But they don't know the right questions to uncover real needs
  • They miss insights, pain points, and workarounds
  • They mistake opinions for evidence
  • They leave interviews with thin notes and no clear path forward
The solution

A copilot that listens, extracts, and guides

The copilot runs alongside the interviewer in real time:

  • Transcribes the conversation live
  • Surfaces the right next question based on what was just said
  • Extracts insights, pain points, frictions, and workarounds
  • Structures everything into a shared evidence pool
  • The human stays in control — the AI handles the cognitive load
The pipeline

From interview to scored ideas

1 Interview
↓
2 Extract insights, pains, workarounds
↓
3 Identify JTBD opportunities
↓
4 Generate ideas
↓
5 Score against Real / New / Good / Feasible
↓
6 Pick the best → build prototype
↓
7 Go back to the person → get feedback

Each interview produces structured evidence. Insights become opportunities. Opportunities become ideas. Ideas are scored against the judging criteria. The best one gets built — then validated with the person you interviewed.

The top idea · 18/20
Promise accountability
1. ProofPass
18/20
Turns an event advertisement into a public promise receipt. Organizers identify the specific experiences they are selling — a workshop, cultural demonstration, or local host — and attach recent, time-stamped proof. Attendees confirm each promise after the event instead of leaving one vague star rating.

Core loop

  1. Paste an event link or poster — the app extracts concrete promises into a checklist
  2. The organizer supplies recent proof for each item, with provenance and date shown
  3. After attending, people confirm "delivered," "partly delivered," or "missing" — creating a claim-level track record

Most common rebuttal

"I can already ask a friend or read Facebook comments."

Friends and comments give scattered anecdotes. ProofPass makes the exact advertised claims comparable before the trip and accountable afterward — while still showing uncertainty when evidence is thin.

Interview signal: the Japanese festival promised culture but felt like a food festival with contemporary performance. The mismatch was between a specific expectation and what was actually delivered.
