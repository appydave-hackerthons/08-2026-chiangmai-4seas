## What this changes

**1 · "Elaborations" is new.** The plan goes fact sheet → candidate opportunities directly. The
board inserts a widening step first. That is the right instinct: the two rows of cards beside it
are a deliberately large search space, and you cannot narrow well from a set that was never wide.
Worth adding to the pipeline properly.

**2 · Judging is its own stage, not a scoring column.** Drawn as a distinct step with its own
output — which matches Karlen's ask for a defined bar rather than a weighted average.

**3 · The D/E/K lanes run the full height.** Ownership is per-stage across the whole funnel, not
"Karlen does discovery, David and Ethan do build". The role split we recorded is coarser than what
the board draws.

**4 · The funnel narrows ~18 → ~5 → built.** Consistent with the plan's honest success bar of
three-to-five runnable apps, arrived at independently.
