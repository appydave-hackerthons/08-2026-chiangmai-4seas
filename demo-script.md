**Beat 1.2 — The artifact demo (2-4 min)**

## Layer 2 — How it meets the needs (the value)

**Goal:** Make it matter. Show the people.

**Beat 2.1 — The person (1 min)**
> "Meet [Person]. They're stuck on [X]."
> [Show their stuck point quote — verbatim]

**Beat 2.2 — The pattern (30 sec)**
> "It wasn't just [Person]. [N of 5] people we talked to were stuck on the same thing."
> [Show the pattern across interviews]

**Beat 2.3 — The bridge (30 sec)**
> "We don't tell people what to do. We help them figure out what they're stuck on — then nudge one small step."
> [Connect the artifact to the need]

## Layer 3 — How we built it (the factory)

**Goal:** Show this is repeatable. Win the meta.

**Beat 3.1 — The method (1 min)**
> "And here's the part we want to highlight: we didn't just build a thing. We built a factory."
> [Show the interview script]
> [Show the decision tree]

**Beat 3.2 — The roles (30 sec)**
> "Ethan ran interviews. Dave built. Karlen wove the story."
> [Reference the role split]

**Beat 3.3 — The closer (30 sec)**
> "Run it again next month on a different theme. Same factory. Different city. Different problem. That's the leverage."
