# Interview Script — Ethan (5-min dynamic interview)

**Goal:** Surface what the person is stuck on. Not what they want — what they're stuck on.

**Length:** 5 minutes, no longer.

**Style:** Dynamic. Follow the workaround thread. Don't read questions in order.

---

## Opening (warm-up, 1 min)

Start with one of these:

- "Tell me about the last thing you did just for you. Not for work. Not for someone else. Just for you."
- "What did you do this weekend that had nothing to do with productivity?"
- "What's something small you did recently that you actually enjoyed?"

**Listen for:** the pause, the hesitation, the "hmm." That's data.

---

## Avoidance (the first 5 questions)

When they start giving surface answers, deploy these:

1. "What's something you keep meaning to do but haven't?"
2. "When did you last say yes to something you didn't want to do?"
3. "What do you do when you're bored but don't want to admit it?"
4. "What's the last thing you bought just for yourself?"
5. "What's something you pretend to like but don't really?"

**Listen for:** the friction they name without realizing it.

---

## Workaround (the gold — spend most time here)

6. "Walk me through how you solved [problem they mentioned]."
7. "What's a small ritual you have that you didn't plan?"
8. "Where do you go when you need to think?"
9. "What's the first thing you do when you wake up?"
10. "What's the last thing you do before sleep?"

**The pattern to follow:** "You said [X]. How does that work for you?" Keep pulling on the workaround thread. Don't move on until you get the texture.

---

## Gap (when workaround is exhausted)

11. "Tell me about a time you almost did something and then didn't."
12. "What's a memory from the last month that surprised you?"
13. "When did you last feel like yourself?"
14. "What's something you wish you could tell people but don't?"
15. "If you had an hour with no obligations, what would you actually do?"

---

## Failed-change (when you need the stuck point surfaced)

16. "Tell me about a time you tried to change a habit and failed."
17. "What did you blame it on? Was that the real reason?"
18. "What would have made it stick?"

**This is the moment.** "What would have made it stick" usually reveals the smallest step.

---

## Closing (transition to extraction)

Ask ONE of these to surface the smallest step:

- "What's the smallest thing that would make tomorrow better?"
- "If you did one thing tomorrow — just one — what would it be?"

**Write down their exact words.** That's the smallest step.

---

## Notes to Ethan (during the interview)

- **Don't ask hypotheticals.** "What would you do if..." → bad. "Tell me about a time you..." → good.
- **Don't paraphrase.** Write down exact words, especially the workaround.
- **Don't fill silence.** They need 3-5 seconds to think. Wait.
- **Don't move on if they're going deep.** Sit in the workaround thread. The idea lives there.
- **Don't judge.** Whatever they say is data.

---

## After the interview (60 sec capture)

Write down:

- **The stuck point** (one sentence)
- **The smallest step they named** (their exact words)
- **The best quote** (verbatim, with emotion)
- **The workaround** (what they're already doing to cope)
